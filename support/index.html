How do I import a book?

Open an EPUB with Aedo from Files, AirDrop or the Share menu, or import it inside the app.

Why was my EPUB refused?

Aedo reads DRM-free, reflowable EPUBs in English. Files with DRM and fixed-layout books can't be played.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the paywall from Profile and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

How does iCloud Sync work?

Turn it on in Profile, on each device, with the same Apple Account. Books and progress go to your private iCloud storage.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

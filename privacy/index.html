Aedo Privacy Policy

Updated: November 7, 2026

Controller: Andrea Paracino, Via dell'Acquedotto Romano 5, andre.paracino@gmail.com.

Aedo has no account and no server. Your books, listening progress and settings stay on your device. Speech and AI features run on your device.

If you turn on iCloud Sync, they are stored in your private iCloud account, which we cannot read. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see payment details. Aedo may download English language data from Apple. Links to book sources open in your browser.

We collect no analytics and no personal data through the app. If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple sends us the feedback you submit and the crash reports you choose to share, used only to fix the app.

Questions or requests: andre.paracino@gmail.com.
